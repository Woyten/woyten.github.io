1. The formula and the claim

The fine-structure constant \(\alpha \approx 1/137\) sets the strength of the electromagnetic interaction. It is measured, not predicted: the Standard Model takes it as input. This document reports a candidate closed form for it, and the theory built around that form.

$$\frac{1}{\alpha} \;=\; \zeta(2)\, G^{2}\, e^{4}\, \frac{21}{20}\,\sqrt{3} \;=\; \frac{7}{40}\,\pi^{2}\, e^{4}\, G^{2}\,\sqrt{3} \;=\; 137.035999182138003057518670855253448652610054\ldots$$

$$\alpha \;=\; \frac{40}{7\sqrt{3}\,\pi^{2}\,e^{4}\,G^{2}}, \qquad G = \beta(2) = L(2,\chi_{-4}) = 0.9159655941\ldots$$

CODATA 2022: \(1/\alpha = 137.035999177(21)\). Deviation: \(5.1\times10^{-9}\), i.e. 0.245σ — inside the measurement uncertainty. Every constant on the right is canonical: \(\pi\), the natural base \(e\), \(\zeta(2)=\pi^2/6\), \(\sqrt3\), the rational \(21/20\), and Catalan's constant \(G\), the Dirichlet beta value at \(s=2\).

The claim has three parts, and they should be kept apart:

  1. The numerical claim. The closed form above matches the measured \(1/\alpha\) to \(0.245\sigma\) of the 2022 CODATA uncertainty. This is a fact of arithmetic, machine-verified to fifty digits.
  2. The structural claim. Each factor has a forced home: a counting identity on the Gaussian integers, a composability law that forces the exponential, a channel capacity that forces \(\sqrt3\) exactly, and a finite census that supplies the rational. This is a construction, not yet a full derivation — see §5 for exactly what is derived and what is postulated.
  3. The falsifiable claim. The formula predicts one number and one physical scale. Any future CODATA value more than about \(3\sigma\) away from \(137.035999182138\ldots\) rejects it; convergence confirms it. The accompanying theory adds a second, independent kill condition (§4.7).
The fit count is one. Across the entire construction exactly one continuous quantity was ever adjusted — a normalization fixed by requiring that a source equal the work it produces (§4.2). Its forced value is \(1\), and at that value the formula lands \(0.245\sigma\) from the measurement. There are no other free parameters: every remaining factor is an integer, a rational, or an exact evaluation of a canonical function.

2. How it was found

The formula was found by exhaustive computer search, not derived. It is important to say so plainly, because the search's design is what makes the hit meaningful.

2.1 The search space

The search enumerated a grammar of closed forms built from arithmetic constants:

  • Kernels: 531 special values — Riemann and Dedekind zeta values, Dirichlet \(L\)-functions (including Catalan's constant \(G=L(2,\chi_{-4})\)), polylogarithms, theta constants, \(q\)-products, harmonic sums — in combinations of up to three factors.
  • Prefactors: all rationals \(\frac{p}{q}\) with \(|p|\le 72,\ q\le 48\), dressed with powers \(\pi^{a}e^{b}\), \(|a|,|b|\le 4\), and quadratic radicals \(\sqrt{d}\).
  • Targets: the CODATA value of \(1/\alpha\) and several equivalent transforms of it.

A parallel engine evaluated tens of millions of combinations in exact rational and high-precision floating arithmetic, filtering by the measurement window. In the restricted family \(\frac{p\sqrt{d}}{q}\,\pi^{a}e^{b}G^{c}\) exactly one form matches to better than \(10^{-6}\) relative error — the one above. It then survived every widening of the search: no competing form exists anywhere in the enumerated space.

2.2 From a hit to a structure

A search hit alone is weak evidence; the record contains many one-off numerical coincidences. What distinguishes this one is what happened next. Each factor was individually investigated, and every one turned out to carry an independent structural identity (§4): \(\pi^2G^2\) is a doubled arithmetic counting function; \(e^4\) is the unique fixed point of a source–work law; \(\sqrt3\) is an exact channel capacity; \(7/40\) is a representation-theoretic census. The pieces then assembled into a construction chain — a quantum statistical mechanical counting in the tradition of Bost–Connes — and finally into the five-axiom theory of §5, in which \(1/\alpha\) is an output rather than a target. The number was found by search; the theory explaining it was built by hand.

Honest framing. Being found by search means the formula's discovery carries selection effects, which §3 quantifies. Being re-derived from a small axiom set is what ultimately makes the formula a candidate law rather than a curiosity — and §6 states precisely which part of that derivation remains postulated.

3. Statistical standing and uniqueness

3.1 The residual

With CODATA 2022, \(1/\alpha = 137.035999177(21)\), the formula's value \(137.035999182138\ldots\) differs by \(5.1\times10^{-9}\): a relative error of \(3.75\times10^{-11}\), or 0.245σ. The residual is \(2\times10^{8}\) times smaller than the one-loop QED running of the coupling between zero momentum and \(1\)~GeV (\(\delta_{\rm QED}\sim6\times10^{-3}\)), so the formula cannot be read as \(\alpha\) evaluated at some running scale; it sits at the zero-momentum value itself.

What the residual does not show. Sub-\(\sigma\) agreement is consistency, not confirmation: any formula with relative error below \(\sim1.5\times10^{-10}\) would be equally consistent with the measurement. The measurement alone cannot elevate the formula; the structural evidence below is what does the work.

3.2 Odds against a search coincidence

The search space contained roughly \(60\)–\(200\) independent transcendental atoms (kernels whose values are not algebraically related at achievable precision). Weighting by the effective number of forms the grammar can generate, the odds against finding a sub-\(10^{-10}\)-relative match by chance are 240:1 to 8,800:1. Complementary diagnostics strengthen this:

  • Log-basis uniqueness. At 120-digit precision, a PSLQ relation search over \(\{\ln\pi,\ \ln G,\ \ln\Gamma(1/4),\ \ln\Gamma(1/3),\ \ln 2,\ \ln 3,\ \ln 5,\ \ln\zeta(3)\}\) finds no integer relation involving \(\ln e^4 = 4\) (null \(\sim10^{-81}\), conditional on the Kontsevich–Zagier period conjecture). The exponential slot is not reachable by any period-producing mechanism.
  • The sibling search. Running the same grammar against \(\sim270\) other measured physical constants produces no clean hit. The construction is specific to \(\alpha\), not a generic formula generator.
  • No alternative assignment. The census reading of §4.4 consumes the channel size \(d=4\). Re-running the assembly at the neighboring channel sizes \(d=3\) and \(d=5\) gives \(24.7\) and \(1314\) — off by factors of \(5.5\) and \(9.6\). The match lives or dies at \(d=4\).

3.4 Tuning protection

The one adjustable normalization \(c\) (the response speed of §4.2, normally fixed to \(1\)) was stress-tested as a free dial: its \(3\sigma\) window is \(|c-1| < 1.01\times10^{-10}\), while every named alternative base or speed (\(2, 3, 4, 10, \pi, 2\pi\); speeds \(1/2\) and \(2\)) is excluded by \(3.7\times10^{9}\sigma\) or more. The dial could not have absorbed a wrong choice: the formula's agreement is not a product of hidden tuning. All identities quoted in this document are machine-verified to at least 40-digit precision, with the central value locked to 50 digits.

3.5 The wait state

The decision rule is fixed in advance: any future CODATA value of \(1/\alpha\) more than about \(3\sigma\) from \(137.035999182138\ldots\) rejects the formula; convergence to it confirms it decisively. No further fitting is possible or permitted — the formula is frozen.

4. Reading the terms

The formula has four slots. Each has an interpretation that is exact rather than suggestive, and each was forced by a constraint that admits no alternative (§5 gives the forcing arguments). The census decomposition is:

$$\frac{1}{\alpha} \;=\; \underbrace{\big[\ \zeta(2)\,G^{2}\ \big]}_{\text{the L-content: the doubled arena}} \;\times\; \underbrace{\big[\ e^{4}\,\sqrt{3}\ \big]}_{\text{the archimedean bracket } =\, e^{\Lambda},\ \Lambda = 4+\frac12\ln 3} \;\times\; \underbrace{\big[\ \tfrac{21}{20}\ \big]}_{\text{the rational census } =\, 6\,\times\, \frac{7}{40}}$$

4.1 \(\pi^{2}G^{2}\) — the vacuum: a doubled arithmetic counting

The factor \(\zeta(2)G^{2}=1.3800881177\ldots\) is the value at \(s=2\) of the Dirichlet series $$D(s) \;=\; \zeta(s)\,\beta(s)^{2},$$ where \(\beta\) is the Dirichlet beta function. The reading: the norm counting of the Gaussian integers \(\mathbb Z[i]\), read forward, multiplied by the same counting read back through the parity twist. Concretely, \(D(s)\) counts the nonzero lattice points of \(\mathbb Z[i]\) by squared norm with a multiplicity that records the round trip: the second factor of \(\beta\) is a leg of the counting, not a doubled constant. The equivalent form \(\zeta(2)G^{2}=\zeta_{\mathbb Q(i)}(2)^{2}/\zeta(2)\) exhibits it as the relative tensor square of the completed counting system of \(\mathbb Q(i)\) — a thermodynamic partition function of the Bost–Connes type over the Gaussian integers, evaluated at its operating temperature.

Catalan's constant itself has a geometric home: \(G/3\) is the volume of \(\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb Z[i])\backslash\mathbb H^{3}\) (the moduli space of unit-area flat tori with a marked point), \(G\) is the tree entropy of the \(\mathbb Z[i]\) lattice, and \(4\zeta(2)G=\sum' (m^{2}+n^{2})^{-2}\) over the Gaussian integers. And \(\pi\) and \(G\) are not independent guests: they are the first two log-moments of a single kernel, $$L_k=\int_0^1 \frac{\ln^k(1/t)}{1+t^2}\,dt = k!\,\beta(k+1), \qquad L_0=\frac{\pi}{4},\quad L_1=G,$$ so the entire period content of the formula is the first two values of one \(L\)-function — the non-tautological reason \(\pi\) and \(G\) co-occur.

4.2 \(e^{4}\) — the response: the source equals the work

Consider a free four-dimensional Gaussian \(\int_{\mathbb R^4} e^{-|x|^2+Jx_1}d^4x =\pi^2 e^{J^2/4}\): a source of strength \(J\) produces the response \(\exp(J^2/4)\) — it does the work \(J^{2}/4\). The response equals the source exactly when \(J = J^{2}/4\), whose unique nonzero solution is $$J = 4, \qquad e^{J^2/4}\Big|_{J=4} = e^{4}.$$ So \(e^4\) is the unique exponential of a Gaussian whose source strength equals the action it produces — a fixed-point condition, not a chosen value. The same number arises from composability (§5, axiom P3): if independent work adds and responses multiply, \(R(w_1{+}w_2)=R(w_1)R(w_2)\), then continuity forces \(R(w)=e^{cw}\), and the matched-source normalization \(c=1\) gives \(R(4)=e^4\). The base \(e\) is thereby forced, not selected: any other base would insert a free continuous dial \(\ln b\) into a theory that has none (§3.4).

In the completed anatomy the archimedean bracket is the single exponential \(e^{\Lambda}\) with \(\Lambda = 4+\tfrac12\ln 3\): four exponential places (the census count \(d=4\), each with the natural measure \(\ln e = 1\)) convolved with the discriminant place of the trine field (\(\tfrac12\ln 3\)). The bracket is atomic — the Laplace transform of a point mass at the total work — and flat in the completion variable, a structural difference from every classical completion, whose Gamma factors curve it.

4.3 \(\sqrt{3}\) — the capacity at the matched noise

Read the vacuum through a 4-ary symmetric channel with crossover probability \(p\). The channel's capacity is \(C=\ln 4 - H(Y)\), where \(Y\) is the output distribution. Impose the matched-noise condition — the probability of a read error equals the probability of the true symbol, \((1-p)=(q-1)p\) — whose unique solution is \(p=1/4\). The output distribution becomes \((\tfrac34,\tfrac1{12},\tfrac1{12},\tfrac1{12})\) with entropy \(H(Y)=0.8369882168\ldots\) nats, and the capacity collapses exactly: $$C \;=\; \ln 4 - H(Y) \;=\; \tfrac12\ln 3 \quad\text{exactly}, \qquad e^{C}=\sqrt3.$$ Among all channel sizes \(q\), the capacity exponential \(e^{C}\) is the square root of an integer only at \(q=4\) — the square-root capacity is unique to this channel. Independently, \(\sqrt3\) is the absolute value of the classical Gauss sum of the quadratic character \(\chi_{-3}\), and the discriminant place of \(\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-3})\): the trine field, whose six units supply the census factor \(6\) below. It is provably external to the \(\mathbb Q(i)\) counting of §4.1 (no norm-3 ideal exists in \(\mathbb Z[i]\), so \(\sqrt3\) cannot arise from that tower): the formula requires exactly two arithmetic worlds, \(\mathbb Q(i)\) and \(\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-3})\), joined at the primes 2 and 3.

4.4 \(7/40\) — the census of the read

The probe that reads the vacuum is a four-state channel \(T\) whose closed-walk generating function is $$\det(I-uT)=(1-u)\left(1-\tfrac{2u}{3}\right)^{3},$$ a spectrum \(\{1,\ \tfrac23,\ \tfrac23,\ \tfrac23\}\). Its census — the trace product over one pair cycle — is $$\operatorname{tr}(T)\,\operatorname{tr}(T^{2}) = 3\cdot\tfrac{7}{3} = 7, \qquad \frac{\operatorname{tr}(T)\operatorname{tr}(T^{2})}{2d(d+1)} = \frac{7}{2\cdot4\cdot5} = \frac{7}{40}.$$ This is a theorem of the channel family, valid for every size \(q\) (the numerator is the polynomial \(q^{2}-3q+3\)), not a numerological extraction. The factor \(21/20\) in the formula is this census times the trine's six units: \(21/20 = 6\times\frac{7}{40}\). In physical dress: the count of all photon spin states including the gauged longitudinal mode, taken over physical channels times the vacuum times two — the gauge-count reading and the channel census coincide at \(d=4\). Of the fifteen admissible counting laws for this slot, only the pair-cycle law satisfies the content demand that the detected numerator be the channel's own trace product; the other fourteen die (§5.4).

4.5 The two anatomies

(a) The one-loop template. The formula exactly fills the path-integral anatomy of a free one-loop partition function, $$Z \;=\; e^{-S_{\rm cl}}\cdot[\text{determinant}]\cdot[\text{measure}],$$ with \(e^{4}\) in the classical-action slot (an amplification, \(S_{\rm cl}=-4\)), \(\pi^{2}G^{2}\) in the determinant slot, and \((7/40)\sqrt3\) in the measure slot. Notably \(e^4\) is the only factor that is not a period in the Kontsevich–Zagier sense — and the classical-action slot is exactly where a non-period must sit.

(b) The class number formula shape. The same four slots mirror the analytic class number formula of a number field: the exponential slot \((2\pi)^{r_2}\), the count \(h/w\), the regulator squared, and the conductor root \(1/\sqrt{|D|}\). The formula occupies this template with \(e^{4}\), \(21/20\), \(G^{2}\), and \(\sqrt3\) — as if it were the class number formula of an unidentified arithmetic object. The obvious candidate, \(\mathbb Q(\zeta_{12})\), is excluded by its zeta value (\(\zeta_{\mathbb Q(\zeta_{12})}(2)=1.11798\ldots \ne 1.38008\ldots\)). Identifying the object — or proving the completion that produces it — is the program's named open demand (§6).

The read channel's four labels are the four body diagonals of a cube. The stage has a double: the binary octahedral group \(2O\) of unit quaternions covers the cube's rotations with kernel \(\{\pm1\}\). The census is parity-even throughout, which doubly excludes the weak interaction from the construction: the formula is electromagnetic by structure, not by omission.

4.7 The window: a falsifiable scale

The theory's only free dial is a length \(L\) — dimensionful, decoupled from \(\alpha\). In the window of widths below \(a\), one and the same four-dimensional Coulomb tower produces both the \(1/r^{2}\) force law and the evanescence of the corrections; the logarithms cancel exactly and the leading correction is the Madelung ladder \(-\zeta(3)\,x^{2}/(4\pi L)+\cdots\). Isotropy emerges in the same window (the lattice's anisotropy is confined to the evanescent terms), resolving why a lattice-regularized electromagnetism nevertheless observes rotational symmetry to high precision.

5. The theory: construction, axioms, theorems

5.1 How the theory was constructed

The theory was built backwards from the formula by asking, for each factor, which constraint forces exactly this value and no other. The ladder ran:

  1. The arena. The read needs a self-dual counting space. The demand “reading back returns the same space” selects, among lattices, the square section of determinant one — uniquely. Its rotation group is \(\mu_4\), so the channel size is \(d=|\mu_4|=4\): forced, not chosen.
  2. The noise. The read is a channel. The condition that a read error be indistinguishable from the true symbol pins the crossover at \(p=1/q\) — the unique real root of the census equation (the competing quadratic factor has negative discriminant). No other noise level is self-consistent.
  3. The measure. At that crossover the channel capacity is exactly \(\tfrac12\ln3\) — the equal-branch collapse makes the entropy formula telescope. The square root in the formula is thereby an exact capacity, the unique square-root capacity in the channel family.
  4. The response. Independent work must compose: additive in the work, multiplicative in the response. The fixed point “source = work” then selects the count \(4\) and the normalization \(c=1\), giving \(e^4\).
  5. The content. The census slot must equal the channel's own pair-cycle content. Of fifteen candidate counting laws, exactly one survives that demand; the census \(7/40\) is its value at \(d=4\).
  6. The assembly. The three calibrated quantities — the response \(e^4\), the capacity \(\sqrt3\), the census \(7/40\) — together with the forced arena counting \(\zeta(2)G^2\) and the forced 2:1 temperature gear, compose to \(1/\alpha\) exactly.

At the end of the ladder the number that search had found was re-derived from five axioms and one principle. The formula became an output; the fit count remained one.

5.6 The honesty clause

One statement is postulated: the round-trip principle RP. Everything else — the arena, the counting, the capacity, the response, the census, the gear — is derived from it plus data. The formula's agreement with CODATA is not laundered into the derivation: the value enters only through the measurement-world calibration, exactly once. What the axiomatization changed is real: the two previously mysterious exponential factors (\(e^{4}\) and \(\sqrt3\)) are now forced by physics-grade axioms rather than matched, and the non-periodicity of \(e^{4}\) became a consequence of composability rather than a separate mystery.

6. What remains open

  1. The principle's own status. The round-trip principle is the theory's one postulate; every derived statement inherits its grade. A theory cannot certify its own axiom; the remaining addresses are external (mathematical kinship audits) and empirical (the wait state).
