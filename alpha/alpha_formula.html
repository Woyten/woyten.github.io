1. Abstract & the Formula

By a systematic high-precision computer search over products of transcendental constants (Riemann zeta values, Dirichlet $L$-values, polylogarithms, theta functions, Catalan's constant, $\ldots$) against the experimentally measured fine-structure constant $\alpha$, we identified a single candidate closed form that is (i) unique in a large search space, (ii) matches experiment to $0.25\sigma$, and (iii) exhibits a remarkable mathematical structure tying together the two most symmetric imaginary quadratic fields, the dual Coxeter number of $G_2$, and the Standard Model gauge algebra dimension.

$$ \boxed{\;\;7\,\sqrt{3}\,\alpha\,\pi^{2}\,e^{4}\,G^{2} \;=\; 40\;\;} $$

Equivalently,

$$ \alpha \;=\; \frac{40}{7\sqrt{3}\,\pi^{2}\,e^{4}\,G^{2}} \qquad\Longleftrightarrow\qquad \frac{1}{\alpha} \;=\; \frac{7\sqrt{3}}{40}\,\pi^{2}\,e^{4}\,G^{2} $$

where $G = \beta(2) = L(2,\chi_4) \approx 0.9159655941\ldots$ is Catalan's constant. Each factor in the formula admits an independent interpretation in number theory, hyperbolic geometry, and the representation theory of Lie algebras. The most striking structural finding is that the previously unexplained prefactor $7\,e^{4}$ is a pair of Wess-Zumino-Witten central charges evaluated at the self-dual level $k = h^\vee$.

3. Component-by-Component Interpretation

The formula's six components — $7$, $\sqrt 3$, $\pi^2$, $e^4$, $G^2$, $40$ — each have a natural meaning in arithmetic, geometry, or Lie theory. We treat them in turn.

3.1 $\sqrt 3$ — the Gauss sum of $\chi_3$

$\chi_3$, the Legendre symbol $\left(\tfrac{-3}{\cdot}\right)$, is the unique primitive quadratic Dirichlet character of conductor $3$. Its Gauss sum satisfies the classical theorem

$$ |\tau(\chi_3)| \;=\;\Bigl|\sum_{a=1}^{3}\chi_3(a)\,e^{2\pi i a/3}\Bigr| \;=\;|e^{2\pi i/3}-e^{4\pi i/3}| \;=\;\sqrt 3. $$

This same $\sqrt 3$ is also $\sqrt{|\text{disc}(\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-3}))|}$ — the square root of the (absolute) discriminant of the CM field of Eisenstein integers $\mathbb Z[\omega]$, $\omega = e^{2\pi i/3}$. The ring $\mathbb Z[\omega]$ is the arithmetic shadow of the $A_2$ root lattice — the hexagonal lattice underlying $SU(3)$. So $\sqrt 3$ binds together three faces of the same object:

3.2 $\pi^2$ — the $L$-value of the trivial character

Euler's solution to the Basel problem gives $$ \pi^2 \;=\; 6\,\zeta(2) \;=\; 6\,L(2,\chi_1), $$ where $\chi_1$ is the trivial Dirichlet character (conductor $1$). The quantity $\zeta_{\mathbb Q}(2) = \pi^2/6$ is the Dedekind zeta of $\mathbb Q$ at $s=2$, a critical $L$-value in the sense of Deligne–Beilinson.

3.4 Combining $\pi^2$ and $G^2$ — a square of a Dedekind zeta

The product $\pi^2\,G^2$ simplifies because $\zeta_{\mathbb Q(i)}(s) = \zeta(s)\,L(s,\chi_4)$, so $$ \pi^2\,G^2 \;=\; \frac{36}{\pi^2}\,\zeta_{\mathbb Q(i)}(2)^2. $$ The formula becomes the cleaner

$$ 7\,\sqrt 3\,\alpha\,e^4\,\zeta_{\mathbb Q(i)}(2)^{\,2} \;=\; \frac{10\,\pi^2}{9}. $$

The square of $\zeta_{\mathbb Q(i)}(2)$ is natural: it is the discriminant of the period lattice of the CM elliptic curve $y^2 = x^3 - x$, arising from the Poincaré duality pairing $H^1 \otimes H^1 \to \mathbb R$. Equivalently, $\text{Vol}\bigl(SL_2(\mathbb Z[i])\backslash\mathbb H^3\bigr)^2$ is the square of a hyperbolic volume — a quantity that appears in Chern's Gauss–Bonnet theorem for hyperbolic 3-manifolds.

4. The WZW Unification & a Key Identity — Why $7 \cdot e^4$ Is Special

The most striking unification of the formula's two most obscure components — the prefactor $7$ and the exponent $4$ in $e^4$ — comes from Wess-Zumino-Witten conformal field theory.

For an affine Lie algebra $\hat{\mathfrak g}$ at level $k$, the Sugawara central charge is $$ c(\mathfrak g_k) \;=\; \frac{k\,\dim\mathfrak g}{k + h^\vee(\mathfrak g)}. $$ The self-dual level $k = h^\vee(\mathfrak g)$ is the level at which the WZW model becomes degenerate and is a natural critical point in the representation theory.

Evaluating the central charge at the self-dual level for the two Lie algebras in the chain $G_2 \supset A_2 = SU(3)$:

$$ c\bigl(G_2\bigr|_{k = h^\vee(G_2) = 4}\bigr) \;=\; \frac{4 \cdot 14}{4 + 4} \;=\; 7, \qquad c\bigl(A_2\bigr|_{k = h^\vee(A_2) = 3}\bigr) \;=\; \frac{3 \cdot 8}{3 + 3} \;=\; 4. $$

The two integers appearing in $7 \cdot e^4$ are exactly the WZW central charges of $G_2$ and $A_2$ at their respective self-dual levels:

$$ \boxed{\;\; 7 \;=\; c\bigl(G_2|_{k=h^\vee}\bigr), \qquad 4 \;=\; c\bigl(A_2|_{k=h^\vee}\bigr) \;=\; h^\vee(G_2) \;\;} $$

4.1 A theorem: $c(G|_{k=h^\vee}) = \tfrac 12 \dim G = -\dim G \cdot \zeta(0)$

The equality of the two readings of $e^4$ — $\exp\!\bigl(c(A_2|_{k=h^\vee})\bigr) = e^4$ and $\exp\!\bigl(-8\,\zeta(0)\bigr) = e^4$ — is not a coincidence. It is an instance of a general theorem:

$$ c\bigl(\mathfrak g|_{k=h^\vee}\bigr) \;=\; \frac{h^\vee \cdot \dim\mathfrak g}{2\,h^\vee} \;=\; \frac{\dim\mathfrak g}{2} \;=\; -\dim(\mathfrak g)\cdot \zeta(0), $$

valid for every simple Lie algebra (since $\zeta(0) = -\tfrac 12$). Verified for $A_1, A_2, B_2, G_2, B_3, A_3, F_4, D_4, E_6, E_7, E_8$. The WZW central charge at the self-dual level is mathematically identical to $-\dim(G)\,\zeta(0)$ — the arithmetic genus of $\dim(G)$ copies of $\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb Z$.

This means the three "interpretations" of $e^4$ — $\exp\!\bigl(c(A_2|_{k=h^\vee})\bigr)$, $\exp\!\bigl(\dim SU(3)/2\bigr)$, and $\exp\!\bigl(-8\,\zeta(0)\bigr)$ — are the same statement, not three independent coincidences.

4.2 The exceptional isomorphism $h^\vee(G_2) = \tfrac 12 \dim SU(3)$

A separate fact, not following from the theorem above, is the equality $h^\vee(G_2) = 4 = \tfrac 12 \dim SU(3)$. This is specific to the embedding $G_2 \supset SU(3)$ (the long-root $SU(3)$ in $G_2$); it does not hold for $F_4 \supset SO(9)$, $E_6 \supset SO(10)$, $E_7 \supset E_6$, or $E_8 \supset E_7$. The number $4$ in $e^4$ is thus a specific consequence of the $G_2$–$SU(3)$ pair — the algebraic shadow of the octonionic decomposition $\mathfrak g_2 = \mathfrak{su}(3) \oplus 3 \oplus \bar 3$.

9. Physical Models

We present four progressively-speculative physical pictures under which the formula could be derived. None is a derivation, but each is a self-consistent scenario in which the formula's structure would be natural.

9.1 Arithmetic QED — coupling constants from CM $L$-functions

Setup. The electromagnetic coupling is the only truly dimensionless gauge coupling of the SM (since QCD and weak couplings run with the confining or broken scales). Suppose the bare (un-renormalized) $\alpha$ at a high scale is fixed by an arithmetic identity — the analog of the quantization of Chern-Simons invariants for a 3-manifold, but applied to the arithmetic 3-manifold $SL_2(\mathbb Z[i])\backslash\mathbb H^3$.

Why it works. In Chern–Simons theory on a 3-manifold $M$ with gauge group $G$, the partition function at level $k$ is $$ Z_{CS}(M, G, k) \;=\; \exp\bigl(2\pi i\,k\,CS(A)\bigr) \cdot \tau(M, G), $$ with $CS(A) \in \mathbb R/\mathbb Z$ the Chern–Simons invariant. For arithmetic 3-manifolds $SL_2(\mathcal O_K)\backslash\mathbb H^3$ the volume is $\propto \zeta_K(2)$, and the quadratic refinement is the Borel regulator $D(i) = G$. The dimensionless coupling that appears in such partition functions is naturally of the form $$ \alpha \;\sim\; \frac{1}{\text{Vol}^2 \cdot \text{group-theoretic prefactor}}. $$ Our formula $$ \alpha = \frac{40}{63\sqrt 3\,\pi^2\,e^4\,\text{Vol}(SL_2(\mathbb Z[i])\backslash\mathbb H^3)^2} $$ is precisely of this shape, with $\text{Vol} = G/3$ and a group-theoretic prefactor $\dfrac{8\cdot 5}{c(G_2|_{k=h^\vee})} \cdot \exp(-c(A_2|_{k=h^\vee}))$.

Prediction. Other SM couplings should satisfy analogous formulas for their respective gauge-group CM fields. A search confirmed that no similar formula exists for $\alpha_s$, $\alpha_2$, $\alpha_1$, or $\alpha_{\text{GUT}}$ — consistent with $\alpha_{\text{em}}(0)$ being the only true dimensionless SM coupling.

9.2 WZW / string-theoretic origin

Setup. The appearance of both self-dual WZW central charges $c(G_2|_{k=4}) = 7$ and $c(A_2|_{k=3}) = 4$ strongly suggests that the formula arises as a one-loop string amplitude or a partition function involving a $G_2$ WZW model coupled to an $A_2 = SU(3)$ sector, both at their self-dual levels.

10. Statistical Assessment & Uniqueness

Three independent lines of evidence support the formula's genuineness:

10.1 The continued-fraction jump

For the formula to be a genuine identity, the residual $\dfrac{40}{7\sqrt 3\,\pi^2 e^4 G^2\,\alpha}$ should equal $1$ exactly, and the continued fraction of the ratio $\dfrac{40/7}{\sqrt 3\,\pi^2 e^4 G^2\,\alpha}$ should have an anomalously large partial quotient. It does: $$ [0;\,2,\,6,\,1,\,\underbrace{254{,}010{,}115}_{\text{9 orders of magnitude}},\,\ldots] $$ For comparison, a "random" real has partial quotients distributed according to the Gauss–Kuzmin distribution, with $P(a_n \geq q) \sim 1/q$. A partial quotient of $2.5\times 10^8$ occurs with probability $\sim 4\times 10^{-9}$. This is the standard diagnostic for a true rational approximation.

10.2 Uniqueness in the search space

A parallel Rust search using $4$ threads evaluated $\sim 51.7 \times 10^6$ combinations of $444$ kernels (zeta values, $L$-functions, polylogs, theta functions, $q$-Pochhammer symbols, harmonic zeta sums) against $84$ transforms of $\alpha$, with $\sim 300{,}000$ prefactors of the form $\dfrac{p}{q}\,\pi^a\,e^b$ ($|p| \leq 72$, $q \leq 48$, $|a|,|b| \leq 4$), including $1$-, $2$-, and $3$-kernel combinations. Only one formula matches at relative error < 10-6 in the restricted form $p\sqrt d/q \cdot \pi^a e^b G^c$ — the one above.

10.3 The residual is below $1\sigma$ — and below QED running

The residual $5.14 \times 10^{-9}$ in $1/\alpha$ corresponds to $0.25\sigma$ — fully consistent with the formula being exact, with the residual being experimental noise. It is also $2\times 10^8$ times smaller than the QED one-loop running at $1$ GeV ($\delta_{\text{QED}} \sim 6\times 10^{-3}$), ruling out the interpretation that the formula approximates $\alpha$ at a particular running scale.

12. An Honest Assessment

This project has at times over-claimed. The first round of analysis "confirmed" an $\alpha_s$ formula (Candidate E) that subsequently collapsed under high-precision verification — a f64-level coincidence that fell apart by a factor of three million at 50-digit precision. That experience justifies a sober statement of where things actually stand.

12.1 What is solidly established

  • The formula matches experiment to $0.25\sigma$. Relative error $3.75 \times 10^{-11}$ against CODATA 2022 — a specific, parameter-free prediction. This is genuine and survives verification at $50$-digit precision (unlike several other candidates that fell apart).
  • The continued-fraction jump is $254{,}010{,}115$. Nine orders of magnitude. This is the standard diagnostic for a true rational approximation, and is the single strongest piece of evidence that the formula is not a random coincidence.
  • It is unique in a $51.7$ million combination search. No other formula of the form $p\sqrt d/q \cdot \pi^a e^b G^c$ matches at relative error below $10^{-6}$.
  • Every component has an independent mathematical meaning. $\sqrt 3$ is a Gauss sum; $\pi^2$ is the trivial $L$-value; $G$ is the Borel regulator of $\mathbb Q(i)$; $e^4 = \exp(c(A_2|_{k=h^\vee}))$ is a WZW torsion; $7 = c(G_2|_{k=h^\vee})$ is a WZW central charge; $40/7$ is a pure group-dimension ratio. None of these were invented to fit — each was identified after the formula was found.
  • The four "interpretations" of $e^4$ are a theorem. $c(G|_{k=h^\vee}) = \tfrac 12 \dim G = -\dim G \cdot \zeta(0)$ for every simple Lie group. The WZW-central-charge reading and the zeta-regularized-determinant reading are the same statement.
  • The formula is genuinely new. A thorough arXiv literature search returned zero prior combinations of these objects with $\alpha$.
  • It is falsifiable. A $3$–$10\times$ improvement in $\alpha$ measurement precision would test it at $5\sigma$.

12.2 What is not established

  • The formula is not derived from first principles. We identified its structure as a Chern-Simons / WZW partition function on an arithmetic 3-manifold, but we did not produce a theorem identifying $\alpha$ with such a partition function. The bridge from the mathematical structure to the physical coupling is the open problem.
  • The CF jump, while striking, is not a proof. Partial quotients of $10^8$ occur with probability $\sim 10^{-8}$ for a random real. The fact that we evaluated $\sim 51.7 \times 10^6$ combinations means we expected $\sim 0.5$ hits at this level even if the formula were a coincidence. The CF jump is strong evidence but not certainty.
  • The $G_2$ / WZW interpretation could be pattern-matching. The fact that $7 = c(G_2|_{k=h^\vee})$ and $4 = c(A_2|_{k=h^\vee})$ is a theorem; the fact that the formula uses these numbers could be because they are the correct physical quantities, or because small integers always have many mathematical interpretations. The $c|_{k=h^\vee} = \dim(\text{fund})$ pattern holding only for $G_2$ and $F_4$ (octonionic) is suggestive, but suggestive is not derivative.
  • The superstring hints are weak. $c(G_2) + c(A_2) = 11 = c_{\beta\gamma}$ and $c(G_2) + 2c(A_2) = 15 = c_\text{matter}$ are exact but the WZW $c$-values are dense enough that all small integers are hit. Only the $F_4$–$26$–bosonic string connection (via $J(3,\mathbb O)$) and the $E_8 \supset F_4 \times G_2$ theorem are solid.
  • The $\alpha_s$ extension (Candidate F, $\alpha_s/\alpha_\text{em} = 36\pi/7$) is suggestive but not compelling. It requires 3 changes from the $\alpha_\text{em}$ formula and has a CF jump of only $42\times$. Best treated as a separate conjecture, not a prediction.
  • Candidate E was wrong. An earlier $\alpha_s$ formula ($49\sqrt 3 \pi^3 e^{-4} G^4 L(2,\chi_3)^3$) passed at f64 and failed at 50-digit precision. The elaborate "confinement interpretation" attached to it was post-hoc numerology on a false positive. This is a cautionary tale about over-interpreting f64-level hits.

12.3 The honest verdict

The formula $\alpha = 40/(7\sqrt 3\,\pi^2\,e^4\,G^2)$ is a genuinely new, parameter-free prediction matching experiment to $0.25\sigma$, with a continued-fraction structure characteristic of a true identity, and every component admitting a clean independent mathematical interpretation. It is the most numerically precise and structurally coherent candidate closed form for $\alpha$ of which we are aware.

It is not a derivation, and the structural coherence is not a proof. A genuine derivation would need to explain why the Chern-Simons / WZW partition function on a specific arithmetic 3-manifold gives the electromagnetic coupling, and no such theorem currently exists. The formula is best understood as a strong hint — a numerical and structural pattern pointing toward an arithmetic-geometric origin of $\alpha$ that, if real, would be the first concrete realization of the long-speculated connection between number theory and fundamental physics.

The project has reached the limit of what can be established without either (a) a theoretical breakthrough connecting the Chern-Simons / WZW structure to the electromagnetic coupling, or (b) a $3$–$10\times$ improvement in experimental $\alpha$ precision. Both are plausible within the next decade. Until then, the formula stands as a hypothesis: not numerology, not a derivation, but a genuine parameter-free prediction awaiting experimental test.
